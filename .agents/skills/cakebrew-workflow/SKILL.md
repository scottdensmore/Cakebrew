# Cakebrew Workflow

Read the repository's `AGENTS.md` completely before acting. It is the sole
project authority; this skill is a routing adapter. Follow its **Role orchestration and evidence**
section and the assigned role's evidence contract, not a separate checklist here.

Dispatch the configured roles in AGENTS.md lifecycle order with its assignment and handoff contracts. Keep requirements, cross-role decisions and delivery with the primary agent. Route findings through its fix loop and track which evidence is current; use its concurrency rules before overlapping work.
