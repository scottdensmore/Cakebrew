# Cakebrew Workflow

Read the repository's `AGENTS.md` completely before acting. It is the sole
project authority; this skill is a routing adapter. Follow its **Role orchestration and evidence**
section and the assigned role's evidence contract, not a separate checklist here.

Dispatch the configured roles using AGENTS.md's goal authority, lifecycle and
handoff contracts. Schedule independent work in parallel under its isolation
rules while preserving each slice's gate order. Keep requirements, cross-role
decisions and delivery with the primary agent. Route findings through its fix
loop, track current evidence and continue to the authorized goal's completion
without repeated stage approvals; honor its stopping conditions and user limits.
